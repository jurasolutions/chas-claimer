## 6. Defects

No production code or tests were changed in this run (the brief was read-only). Each defect comes
with a proposed failing test for the developer to write first.

### N2 Major: the batch-approve warnings dialog doesn't fit the screen and doesn't scroll

- **Repro:** as the reviewer, select 3 ready claims that have warnings and choose Approve. Try at
  1280×720, then at 360×800.
- **Expected:** the dialog fits within the viewport, or scrolls inside itself, so × , Cancel and
  "Approve anyway" can all be reached.
- **Actual:** at 1280×720 the dialog is 1268px tall. At 360×800 it's 1021px tall with its top at −111px.
  × sits at −79px, Cancel at 783–825px and "Approve anyway" at 837px, below the fold. `overflowY` is
  visible, `maxHeight` none, and the wheel doesn't scroll it. Escape and Tab work, so keyboard users
  get through, but a mouse or touch user can't reach the confirm button. `n2-360-batch-dialog.png`,
  `n2-batch-approve-dialog-overflow.png`.
- **Cause:** the DS `Dialog` sets no max-height or overflow. `src/dialog.tsx`,
  `src/ui/components/BatchApprove.tsx` lines 105–130.
- **Test:** in `src/ui/components/BatchApprove.test.tsx`, render with 3 warned claims and assert the
  dialog body has a bounded height with `overflow-y: auto` (jsdom can't do layout, so assert the
  style or class). Add a Playwright check later that the confirm and close buttons are in the
  viewport at both sizes.

### N1 Minor (accessibility): focus falls to the body after an action replaces its button

- **Repro:** by keyboard, record a portal reference (or withdraw, "Approve anyway", Portal refused,
  Reopen, Acknowledge, "I've uploaded the file", accept a short payment, confirm a hand match, open
  the checklist link, Show NRIC).
- **Expected:** focus moves to the new status message or heading.
- **Actual:** `document.activeElement` is the body. The next Tab starts again from mid-page.
- **Test:** in `src/ui/pages/ClaimDetail.test.tsx`, after recording a reference, expect
  `document.activeElement` not to be `document.body` and to be the status or heading.

### N3 Minor: a frozen withdrawn claim's Who pays panel uses today's settings

- **Repro:** open withdrawn Flexi claim 54d4a8c7 (D04b, withdrawn by the waterfall reorder).
- **Expected:** the header and the split agree. The claim's own share is $400.00.
- **Actual:** the header says $400.00, but Who pays shows CHAS $620.00, Meridian $688.00 and
  Flexi $0.00.
- **Cause:** the split is worked out from current settings (`server/services/claims.ts`, around line
  578), not from the frozen build.
- **Test:** a server test that freezes a claim, changes the waterfall, and asserts the claim's own
  segment equals `claimedCents`.

### N5 Minor: portal reference uniqueness and matching are case-sensitive

- **Repro:** record MHCP-0001 on one claim, then "mhcp-0001" on another.
- **Expected:** refused as `reference_taken`. A remittance line with either case matches the claim.
- **Actual:** accepted. Both claims carry the "same" reference.
- **Cause:** trigger `submissions_reference_once` in `migrations/0001_init.sql` lines 170–180,
  `server/services/claims.ts:436`, `src/domain/matcher.ts:70`.
- **Test:** in the submissions API test, record "MHCP-0001" then "mhcp-0001" and expect 409
  `reference_taken`. Add a matcher unit test that matches across case.

### N6 Minor (copy): finished claims still show the deadline finding

- **Repro:** open paid claim 3ee16c55, or ec015c9c, or SC-F0021.
- **Expected:** no "submit soon" finding once a claim is submitted, paid or rejected.
- **Actual:** "Last 2 days to submit. Approve and submit it soon." Submitted D08b also carries
  `deadline_approaching`. `b10-360-claim.png`.
- **Test:** a rules test that a claim whose status is not unsubmitted gets no `deadline_approaching`.

### N7 Minor: a claim-level pre-auth number doesn't clear `pre_auth_missing`

- **Repro:** add pre-auth PA-TEST-0001 on an insurer claim that has no visit-level pre-auth.
- **Expected:** the `pre_auth_missing` finding clears, as it does on the packet.
- **Actual:** the finding stays, while the packet already uses the claim's number (and prefers it
  over the visit's PA-0016).
- **Cause:** `src/domain/claims/insurance.ts:33` reads only the visit, while `src/domain/packet.ts`
  lines 167–172 prefer the claim annotation.
- **Test:** in `src/domain/claims/insurance.test.ts`, a claim with a pre-auth annotation produces no
  `pre_auth_missing`.

### N4 Minor (copy): history shows "Withdrawn: settings"

- **Repro:** open the history of a claim withdrawn by a settings rebuild.
- **Expected:** plain words, for example "Withdrawn after a settings change".
- **Actual:** "Withdrawn: settings". `src/ui/components/ClaimHistory.tsx:116` prints the raw
  RebuildReason (`server/services/rebuild.ts:43`).
- **Test:** in `ClaimHistory.test.tsx`, a `settings` rebuild reason renders as a sentence.

### N8 Note / question: a short or implicit acceptance closes a claim with no shortfall cue

- **What I saw:** SC-F0097 shows "$70.50 Paid" after a $20.50 payment. The trail reads "Accepted by
  the payment at $20.50". This is by design (`decisions-g2.md:27`, `GLOSSARY.md:150`).
- **The question:** the accept-short dialog doesn't say how much is being given up, and the claim
  page doesn't show it after. Should the dialog and the paid state name the difference?
