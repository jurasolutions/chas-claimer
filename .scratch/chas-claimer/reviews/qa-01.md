## 6. Defects

Severity: **major** blocks an acceptance criterion or a keyboard/screen-reader user; **minor** is
wrong but has a workaround; **note** is worth a follow-up, not a release blocker.

### D01 Major: no filter controls on the claims queue (ticket 02)
- **Repro:** open Claims as the officer. Look for a way to show only `ready`.
- **Expected:** controls on the page for status, scheme, payer, branch, due and changed (technical.md:614, UAT 3.3).
- **Actual:** filtering works only by editing the URL (`?status=ready`). Nothing on the page sets it.
- **Test:** `src/ui/pages/Queue.test.tsx`, `it.fails` "offers a control on the page to filter the claims by status".

### D02 Major: visit notes are computed but never shown (ticket 03)
- **Repro:** open D05's CHAS claim (also D04a, D14, D15, D24, withdrawn D07 after a re-import).
- **Expected:** the visit says the crown is held until complete, why there's no Flexi-MediSave claim, the PA balance warning, and the withdrawn-visit note (UAT 4.2, 4.3, 4.8, 4.10, 5.15).
- **Actual:** none of it appears. `buildVisitClaims` computes notes (`src/domain/claims/build.ts:122-123`), but `server/services/rebuild.ts` keeps only `built.claims`, and `describeVisitNote` (`src/domain/claims/findings.ts:284`) has no caller.
- **Test:** `functions/api/claims/[id]/index.test.ts`, `it.fails` "D05: the claim detail says the crown in progress is held until it's complete".

### D06 Major (accessibility): dialogs don't take focus (ticket 04; root in the DS `Dialog`)
- **Repro:** on a ready claim, focus "Withdraw" and press Enter.
- **Expected:** focus moves into the dialog, Tab stays inside it, and closing it returns focus to "Withdraw".
- **Actual:** the dialog has `aria-modal="true"` but focus stays on the button behind it, and Tab moves through the page underneath. The same applies to every `ClaimActions` dialog, the Portal refused dialog, Acknowledge and the simulator's Reject. `src/design-system/components/feedback/Dialog.jsx` (read-only) handles Escape only. Fix in an app wrapper in `src/ds.ts`, or upstream in the design system and re-sync.
- **Test:** `src/ui/pages/ClaimDetail.test.tsx`, `it.fails` "moves focus into the withdraw dialog when it opens".

### D18 Major: "due within 5 days" counts claims whose window already closed (ticket 06)
- **Repro:** sign in as the reviewer and open the overview. Then open `/claims?scheme=chas&due=5`.
- **Expected:** only CHAS claims with 0 to 5 days left (AC-8.3, functional.md:511). D19's scaling (2 days left) is in; past-window claims are out.
- **Actual:** 113, of which about 103 are past the window. `server/repo/overview.ts` and `server/repo/claims.ts:280` both use `deadline_date <= today + 5` with no lower bound.
- **Test:** `functions/api/overview.test.ts`, `it.fails` "AC-8.3: a CHAS claim whose window already closed is not counted as due within 5 days".

### D09 Minor, borderline major: re-opening your own checklist breaks the first tab (ticket 07)
- **Repro:** open D01's checklist in tab A, then again in tab B (or reload). Record a reference in tab A.
- **Expected:** tab A's submit succeeds. Re-opening a hold you already have doesn't change the claim.
- **Actual:** version goes 3 → 4. Tab A gets "This claim changed while you had it open. Check it and try again."
- **Test:** `functions/api/claims/[id]/checkout.test.ts`, `it.fails` "lets the officer who holds the checklist open it again without moving the claim on".
