# AI proposes; rules and people decide; nothing identifying reaches the model

The claims desk uses Claude for four jobs where the input is messy text: suggesting a code-map entry from a treatment description, explaining a payer's rejection and proposing the fix, drafting a clinical justification for an above-benchmark fee, and proposing a source mapping from the clinic database's column names. Eligibility, amounts and statuses are decided only by the rule sets and by people. Every AI output is a suggestion a person accepts or edits. Requests carry de-identified text only: no NRIC/FIN, name, phone, address, policy or member number, and no database rows. The features fail closed (they report "not configured") when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is unset, and the rest of the desk keeps working.

Amended after ticket 02a's review. The justification draft is the one feature that reads clinical free text. It opens the visit's sealed notes, scrubs them as ADR 0006 describes, and a test asserts the outgoing request body holds none of the visit's known identifiers.

Amended after ticket 09's review. The gate removes the visit's own known identity values in every common written form (a date of birth as "04/03/1960" or "4 Mar 1960" as well as ISO), then refuses to send anything still matching an NRIC/FIN, phone, email or a labelled birth date. One limit is known and accepted: a third party's name in the notes (a relative, a referring doctor) is not a value the desk holds, so the gate cannot recognise it as a name. Clinics are asked to keep third-party names out of notes; the risk is small because the text carries no identifier that ties it to a patient.
