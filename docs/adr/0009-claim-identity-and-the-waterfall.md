# One claim per visit per payer; resubmission is an attempt; submitted claims are fixed inputs

A claim is identified by (visit, payer). MediSave is two payers, Flexi-MediSave and surgical (TOSP) MediSave, because they go through different portals with different rules and can both apply to one visit. Resubmitting a rejected claim adds a submission attempt to the same claim rather than creating a new one, so the audit trail and remittance matching stay on one identity. When a visit is rebuilt, any claim already submitted keeps the amount it claimed and the payers below it in the waterfall are computed from that amount; the submitted claim is flagged changed after submission instead. Every submitted claim comes off the visit before any unsent one, wherever it sits in the waterfall, so a reorder after a lower payer was sent can never claim the same dollars twice (QA 03 D01): the unsent claim gets what the sent ones left, with a `lower_payer_sent_first` warning, and sent claims that add up to more than the visit's fee raise a blocking `sent_claims_exceed_fee` on every unsent claim of the visit, which stays on the desk at $0 until the sent ones are amended, and as a remark on the visit that the sent claims' pages show (QA 03 B1). Approval is bound to the revision it approved: a rebuild that changes the claim clears the approval. When a claim is rejected, its sibling claims get an `upstream_rejected` warning. This is hard to reverse because the unique key, the audit trail and matching all hang off claim identity.
